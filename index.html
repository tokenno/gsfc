The Gavdos SeaFront Collective emerges from the southernmost shore — the pine‑clad cove of Lavraka. For more than thirty years, a community of people has lived in self‑built huts, sharing a deep ethics of care for the land and sea. In the face of tourism pressure and administrative erasure, we gather to document, defend, and continue the laografia tou tora (folklore of the now). This site is our common base: to share observations, archive scientific work, and announce collective actions that protect both the wild character of Gavdos and the right to live simply, without harm.
